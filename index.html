Features

This is Home.

Everything you'd expect from a console, on the PC you already have. It works with a controller, a keyboard or a mouse.

Welcome

Everything at a glance.

Your controller, the free space, what's downloading and whether an update is ready, all before you pick a game. Then your games follow across the top, newest first.

Game Hub

You know before you press Play.

Every game gets its own page: how well it runs, which versions you have, trailers and screenshots, its size and where its files are.

Game Library and Store

PS5, PS4 and PC, in one place.

Your Game Library holds what's installed and what's on its way. The Store helps you discover new games and browse the rest, sorted and filtered from one panel. PS5 games run on KytyPS5 and PS4 games on shadPS4, which SwarmPlay installs and keeps up to date for you. PC games run natively, or with Wine and Proton.

Control Center

One button away.

Press the controller's home button for downloads, notifications and power, from anywhere in SwarmPlay.

You

Everyone gets their own.

Press your picture for your profile: what you're playing, your hours, and a quick way to switch user, open your screenshots or turn off.

Settings

Settings you already know.

Sound, storage for each drive, theme and notifications, laid out like a console's. Add a drive, move games between drives or delete them, all with a controller.

Any controller

DualSense, DualShock 4, Xbox and others, over Bluetooth or USB. In Windows games a DualShock works as an Xbox controller.

Up to date by itself

SwarmPlay tells you when a new version is out and updates in place. Its emulators update too, without touching your saves.

Notifications

When a download finishes, a game is ready or space runs low. Keep them all in one list, or turn on Do not disturb.

Your look

Pick a wallpaper and an accent colour. A game's own art always shows behind it.

Made for Bazzite

Works on Fedora Atomic desktops such as Bazzite, as well as Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch and SteamOS.

Light and fast

Written in Rust, so it opens in a moment and stays out of your game's way.

Install

Ready in a minute.

Open a terminal and run one line. SwarmPlay then shows up in your app menu.

$ curl -fsSL https://github.com/swarmplayhq/swarmplay/releases/latest/download/swarmplay-linux-x86_64.tar.gz | tar xz && ./swarmplay-linux-x86_64/install.sh

It downloads the latest version, puts it in ~/.local/bin and adds it to the app menu. No root password needed.

swarmplay-linux-x86_64.tar.gz

Unpack it and run install.sh in the folder. To remove SwarmPlay later, run install.sh --uninstall; your settings stay.

  • 64-bit Linux from 2022 on: Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36, Bazzite, Arch, SteamOS 3 or newer.
  • A graphics card with Vulkan drivers, for PS5 and PS4 games.
  • Your games. Add the folders where they are; SwarmPlay finds them.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's no account to make and no ads. Install it and play.

Do I need a PlayStation?

No. PS5 games run on the KytyPS5 emulator and PS4 games on shadPS4, right on your PC. SwarmPlay sets both up for you. You bring the games you own.

Will every game run?

Not yet. PS5 and PS4 emulation is still being worked on, so some games don't start or don't get far. Each game's Hub shows how far it gets, so you know before you press Play. PC games show their ProtonDB rating.

How does it update?

SwarmPlay checks for a new version by itself, lets you know with a notification, and updates in place when you say so. Every version is on GitHub.

How is it related to Azrak?

SwarmPlay started as a fork of Azrak Launcher, with a console-style redesign and fixes for Fedora Atomic desktops. It keeps Azrak's folders, so switching keeps your games and settings.

Something doesn't work. Where do I ask?

Open an issue on GitHub and say what you did and what happened. A screenshot helps.
